# KTIP Tester Guide

**Time needed: 30 minutes. No technical skill needed.**

You have been given one role below. Do only your role's steps. Then fill the feedback form inside the app.

---

## Step 0 — Everyone does this first (5 min)

1. Open **[TEST URL]** on your laptop.
2. Click **Sign up**. Use your real email.
3. Pick the role you were assigned (see your section below).
4. Check your email, click the verify link.
5. Finish the **onboarding** questions (country, interests, skills).
6. Land on **Discover**. Look around for 1 minute. Do not click anything yet.

**Write down:** Did anything confuse you in the first minute?

---

## Step 1 — Do your role's tasks (20 min)

Find your name on the assignment sheet, then do that section only.

---

### 🅰 Role A — Student / Youth Innovator

1. Go to **Projects** → open any project → **Like** it, leave a **comment**.
2. Click **+ New Project**. Create a fake project (any idea). Fill title, description, category, phase = Concept. Add a cover image. **Publish**.
3. Go to **Events** → open an event → **Register**.
4. Go to **Resources** → open a resource → download or open it.
5. Go to **Leaderboard** → find your own name.
6. Go to **Dashboard** → check **Achievements** and **Progress** tabs. Did your new project show up?
7. Go to **CV** → click **Edit** → add one experience → save → view public CV.

**Watch for:** anything that fails to save, loads slow, or looks broken on your screen.

---

### 🅱 Role B — Entrepreneur / SME

1. Create a project (same as A step 2) but set phase = **Prototype** and add a funding need.
2. Go to **Grants** → open a grant → click **Apply** → fill and submit the application.
3. Go to **Dashboard → Funding** and **Dashboard → Submissions**. Is your application there?
4. Go to **Settings → Verification**. Start business/SME verification. Upload any document.
5. Go to **Directory** → find another tester → send them a **message**.
6. Open **Messages**. Reply to whoever wrote you.

**Watch for:** confusing form fields, missing confirmation after submitting.

---

### 🅲 Role C — Mentor

1. Go to **Directory** → filter by Students → open 2 profiles.
2. Send a **connection request** to 2 testers.
3. Accept any connection request you receive (**Invitations**).
4. Go to **Dashboard → Mentees**. What do you see?
5. Go to **Forums** → open a board → create a **new post** → reply to someone else's post.
6. Go to **Projects** → comment useful advice on 2 projects.

**Watch for:** is it clear how to actually start mentoring someone?

---

### 🅳 Role D — Investor / Funding Agency

1. Go to **Projects** → use filters (category, phase, country). Try to find fundable projects.
2. Bookmark / save 3 projects.
3. Open a project → contact the owner via **message**.
4. Go to **Grants** → read 2 grants. Would you know how to publish your own?
5. Go to **Dashboard → Funding**.
6. Go to **Leaderboard** and **Discover** — is it clear who the top innovators are?

**Watch for:** do you have enough info on a project page to judge it? What is missing?

---

### 🅴 Role E — Faculty / Researcher

1. Go to **Forums** → create a post asking a research question.
2. Go to **Collaborate** → **Documents** → create a document, type a few lines.
3. **Collaborate** → **Whiteboard** → create one, draw something.
4. **Collaborate** → **Video** → start a call. (Get a second tester to join if possible.)
5. Go to **Dashboard → Research**.
6. Go to **Resources** → try to submit/suggest a resource.

**Watch for:** does collaborate stuff save? Does it work when 2 people are inside at once?

---

### 🅵 Role F — Event / Challenge participant

1. Go to **Events** → open a **Challenge** event.
2. Read the **Challenge Brief**. Download the attached **documents**.
3. **Register** for the event.
4. Submit a **solution** to the challenge (link + description + file).
5. Check the event **Schedule** and **Updates** tabs.
6. Go to **Dashboard → Events** and **Dashboard → Submissions**. Is your submission there?

**Watch for:** did the file upload work? Did you get a confirmation?

---

## Step 2 — Break it on purpose (3 min, everyone)

1. Open the site on your **phone**. Load Discover, Projects, your Dashboard. Anything squashed or cut off?
2. Submit a form with a **blank required field**. Does it warn you clearly?
3. Refresh a page mid-way. Do you lose your work?
4. **Log out**, log back in. Does everything still look right?

---

## Step 3 — Give feedback (2 min, everyone)

Click the **Feedback button** (bottom right corner of any page) → fill the **UAT form** → submit.

Answer honestly. Low scores are more useful than polite ones.

If you found a bug, also write it here in this format:

```
Page: (e.g. Projects → New Project)
What I did: (clicked Publish)
What I expected: (project appears)
What happened: (spinner forever)
Device/browser: (iPhone Safari / Windows Chrome)
Screenshot: (attach)
```

---

## Rules

- Use **fake but realistic** content. No real personal data.
- Do **not** delete other testers' stuff.
- If something breaks, take a screenshot **before** refreshing.
- Stuck for more than 2 minutes? That is a finding. Write it down and move on.
